# Artist Errata

Artists formerly listed in `ARTIST_IMAGE_NOWIKI.md` whose Wikipedia pages were found by hand (the automatic matcher in fetch_data.py missed them because Wikipedia titles the page differently, the museum's spelling differs, or the name lands on a namesake/disambiguation page), plus museum-side duplicate/alternate artist entries for people the collection already lists under another spelling or name (their Notes say which entry is canonical). Each hand-found match is recorded in `MANUAL_WIKI_MATCHES` in wiki_match.py and applied to the app's data files; the Notes line keeps the rationale for the match. Unnumbered, sorted by last name as elsewhere.
