# Artist Errata

Artists formerly listed in `ARTIST_NO_WIKI.md` whose Wikipedia pages were found by hand (the automatic matcher in fetch_data.py missed them because Wikipedia titles the page differently, the museum's spelling differs, or the name lands on a namesake/disambiguation page). Each match is recorded in `MANUAL_WIKI_MATCHES` in fetch_data.py and applied to the app's data files; the Notes line keeps the rationale for the match. Unnumbered, sorted by last name as elsewhere.
