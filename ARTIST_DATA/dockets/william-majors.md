## Wikipedia mentions (up to 5)

- [Spiral (arts alliance)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiral_(arts_alliance)) — listed as a member of the 1963 African-American artists' collective: "William Majors - drawing, painting, paper collages, 3D canvases."
- [Melvin Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melvin_Edwards) — on Edwards's first 1963 trip to New York, "The first person he met at the museum was the artist William Majors, a member of the African-American art group Spiral."
- [John Steptoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Steptoe) — Steptoe "attended the Vermont Academy, where he studied under the sculptor John Torres, and William Majors, a widely acclaimed painter."
- [List of Guggenheim Fellowships awarded in 1974](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Guggenheim_Fellowships_awarded_in_1974) — a 1974 fellow, project described as "Equipping his New Hampshire studio for painting and printmaking."
- [Alvin Hollingsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alvin_Hollingsworth) — "In the summer of 1963, Hollingsworth and fellow African-American artists Romare Bearden and William Majors formed the group Spiral in order to help the Civil Rights Movement through art exhibitions."
