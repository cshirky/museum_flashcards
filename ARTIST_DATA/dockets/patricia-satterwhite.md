## Wikipedia mentions (up to 5)

- [Jacolby Satterwhite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacolby_Satterwhite) — her son's article covers her at length: "His mother was self-taught artist Patricia Satterwhite," whose "schematic drawings/inventions of ordinary objects" his work is built from; it notes she died in 2016, and that his 2019 Pioneer Works show expanded "Patricia Satterwhite's original a cappella recordings from the 1990s." His 2013 solo shows included The House of Patricia, Satterwhite at Mallorca Landings Gallery.
- [Lonnie Holley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lonnie_Holley) — named in the artist list for the Studio Museum in Harlem's 2014 exhibition "When the Stars Begin to Fall: Imagination and the American South," as "Jacolby and Patricia Satterwhite," alongside Thornton Dial, Beverly Buchanan, David Hammons, Kerry James Marshall and Kara Walker.
