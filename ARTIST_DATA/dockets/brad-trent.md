- [List of photographs considered the most important](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_photographs_considered_the_most_important) — credited in the LIFE 100 Photographs table for the 1916 image "Passport of Isadora Duncan": "Copied by Brad Trent, United States." A bare photo credit rather than prose, but he is the only photographer of that name, and the New York editorial photographer (b. 1959) is the Studio Museum's Brad Trent, whose Black Cowboy series showed there in 2016–17.
