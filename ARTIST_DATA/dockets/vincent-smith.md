- [Norman Lewis (artist)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Lewis_(artist)) — a collaborator on the 1974 Printmaking Workshop portfolio Impressions: Our World, Volume I, with "Emma Amos, Benny Andrews, Vivian Browne, Eldzier Cortor, Norman Lewis, Vincent Smith, John Wilson".
