- [Barbara Bullock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Bullock) — listed in the 1975 exhibition "Spirits of Forgotten Ancestors" at the Walnut Street Theater, "paintings, prints and jewelry by Black artists, including Avel de Knight, Bill Howell, Peg Alston and Wendy Wilson."
